Hi! My name is Alexander Bogaars, a Singaporean who's currently in Barcelona, Spain.

I'm a postgraduate student in Economics and finance focused on macroeconomics,
financial markets, and applied data science. I recently graduated with a BSc(hons) in
Economics from Durham University and am now pursuing an MSc in Economics and finance
at the Barcelona School of Economics.

My work interests lie where rigorous econometrics meets macroeconomics and Financial markets. I am interested in working close to home, specifically on the ASEAN+3 region. At Singapore's Economic Development Board, I built models of FDI competitiveness and at Centennial Asia Advisors, I worked on macro research.
